How Skip Tiers Work

Nero queues are ordered by priority brackets, not submission time. A higher tier places your submission in an entirely different priority bracket.

Top Priority

Super Skip

Places you at the absolute top of the queue, bypassing all regular skips and free submissions.

Creator-defined price (e.g. $15 - $25)
Mid Priority

Skip

Jumps over all free submissions. Multiple skip purchasers queue among themselves in arrival order.

Creator-defined price (e.g. $5 - $10)
Base Priority

Free

Standard queue in arrival order alongside everyone else. $0 cost, but no priority.

$0 cost • No priority
* Streamers set their own pricing and names (e.g., "Throne"). * Custom tiers can bundle extra perks (on-camera view, review length).
