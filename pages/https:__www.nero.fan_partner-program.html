Earn 5% of everything your referrals make. For 2 years.

The Nero Partner Program lets anyone earn money by bringing creators to the platform. When you refer an artist who uses Nero, you automatically earn 5% of everything they make—for 2 years.

The artist still keeps their full 80% creator share, and you get 5% on top as a thank you for the referral.

Important: For you to receive commission on a referred user's earnings, they must sign up using your unique referral code. If they sign up without your code, you will not be eligible for partner program benefits for that user.

Here's the math

When someone you refer earns $10,000 through Nero, they take home $8,000 (their 80%), Nero takes $2,000 (the platform fee), and you earn $500 (your 5% referral commission). The more creators you refer, the more streams of passive income you build.

Track everything in real-time

Once you join Nero and get your unique referral code, you'll have access to a dashboard that tracks everything: who you've referred, how much they're earning, what you're making from each referral, and when your payouts are scheduled.

Build your network

The Partner Program creates a win-win situation: creators get a platform to monetize their work, you earn from bringing them on board, and your earnings compound as your network grows.

Whether you're a creator yourself or just know talented artists who livestream, the Partner Program is an opportunity to build recurring revenue.
